# Pilot path acceptance criteria

- Visible after product value/demos and before the final decision-intake CTA.
- Explains a four-stage path: choose, model, test, evaluate.
- Makes a small-scope test feel possible without implying a free engagement or a guaranteed delivery timeline.
- Uses no AI/ML or automation positioning.
- Makes no unsupported ROI/performance claims.
- Links to the localized `/decision` intake.
- Remains readable at mobile widths and with longer UA/PL copy.
