# Conversion review

## Problem
The previous homepage explained QDIP and exposed demos, but the jump from demo exploration to `Describe your decision` was too abrupt for a small-company/NGO buyer. The existing adoption flow described steps but did not answer the commercial objection: *How much do I need to commit before I know whether this helps?*

## Change
The homepage now introduces a scoped pilot path: choose one recurring decision, model the existing decision structure, test representative scenarios, and evaluate usefulness against the current process. A lightweight bridge after the demo section connects examples to the visitor's own decision.

## Why
This lowers perceived adoption risk without using discounts, unsupported ROI claims or promises of a free pilot. It also keeps QDIP positioned as a Decision Engine rather than as automation.
