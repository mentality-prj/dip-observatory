# Public-site conversion funnel

The public QDIP site is optimized for a small-company or NGO decision owner who has a recurring manual decision process and needs a low-risk way to test whether a Decision Engine helps.

## Funnel

1. Recognize a recurring decision problem.
2. Understand how QDIP evaluates alternatives, priorities, constraints and uncertainty.
3. Explore a concrete demo: Resource Allocation or Gas Decision; GTM Lab remains explicitly coming soon.
4. Map the demo pattern to the visitor's own recurring decision.
5. Start with one scoped pilot rather than a platform-wide rollout.
6. Compare QDIP recommendations and evidence with the current decision process.
7. Expand only when practical value is demonstrated.

## CTA policy

- Primary discovery CTA: `See QDIP in action`.
- Demo CTA: open the relevant working demo.
- Conversion CTA: `Describe your decision` / localized equivalent.
- Do not introduce pricing, enterprise rollout, AI/ML, or automation claims into this funnel.
- Keep final authority explicitly with the responsible human.

## Demo policy

Only Resource Allocation, Gas Decision and GTM Lab are marketed as demo patterns. GTM Lab must remain marked as coming soon until its frontend is available.
