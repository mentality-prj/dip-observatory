# Marketing claims guardrails

Until supported by measured production or pilot evidence, do not claim that QDIP:

- reduces decision time by a stated percentage;
- improves outcomes, accuracy or ROI by a stated amount;
- replaces staff or automates an organizational process;
- predicts an outcome with a stated accuracy;
- is powered by AI/ML as its product proposition;
- guarantees an optimal decision.

Safe product claims should describe observable system behavior: QDIP evaluates configured alternatives against priorities and constraints, exposes supporting evidence and uncertainty, provides a recommendation, and leaves the final decision with the responsible person.
