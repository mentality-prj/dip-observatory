# Demo positioning

## Resource Allocation

Primary NGO / constrained-resource example. The buyer question is: **Where should limited resources go?** The demo should show priorities, needs, constraints, alternatives, recommendation and evidence.

## Gas Decision

Commercial changing-conditions example. The buyer question is: **Act now, wait, or choose another strategy?** The demo should demonstrate the same Decision Engine structure in a different domain rather than position QDIP as a forecasting product.

## GTM Lab

Prioritization example. The buyer question is: **Which opportunities deserve attention first?** Keep the public CTA as Coming Soon until the frontend is ready.

Together the three examples communicate ALLOCATE / DECIDE / PRIORITIZE. They are examples of reusable decision structures, not the boundaries of the product.
