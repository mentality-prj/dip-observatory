# Conversion copy principles

- Speak to the decision owner, not to a technical buyer, on the main marketing path.
- Lead with the recurring decision and its operational friction, not QDIP architecture.
- Present demos as examples of decision structures, not as the only supported industries.
- Reduce adoption anxiety: one decision, one pilot, real scenarios, compare with the current process.
- Keep recommendations explainable and human-controlled.
- Avoid AI/ML and automation positioning.
- Avoid unsupported ROI, accuracy, speed or performance claims.
- The technical and research audience continues through QDIP Core rather than adding technical density to the buyer homepage.
