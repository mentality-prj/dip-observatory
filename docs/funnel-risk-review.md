# Funnel risk review

## Risks intentionally avoided

- Too many competing CTAs: discovery stays demo-first; conversion stays decision-intake-first.
- Enterprise anxiety: pilot copy scopes the first step to one recurring decision.
- Product narrowing: demos are presented as decision patterns, not supported-industry boundaries.
- Unsupported trust signals: no fabricated metrics, testimonials or customer logos.
- Positioning drift: no AI/ML or automation proposition.
- Accountability ambiguity: final authority remains with the responsible human.

## Remaining risk

The site still lacks public customer proof. Do not compensate with stronger claims. Add proof only after a pilot/case study is approved for publication.
